# NCTB Secondary Biology — Class 9-10 (জাতীয় শিক্ষাক্রম ও পাঠ্যপুস্তক বোর্ড জীববিজ্ঞান ৯ম-১০ম শ্রেণি)

## Chapter 1: Lessons on Life (জীবন পাঠ)
- Definition & Scope: Biology (Bios = life, Logos = knowledge/study). Physical biology (Morphology, Anatomy, Cytology, Histology, Physiology, Genetics, Ecology) vs Applied biology (Palaeontology, Biostatistics, Parasitology, Fishery, Entomology, Microbiology, Biotechnology).
- Classification of Living Organisms: Purpose of biological taxonomy. Carolus Linnaeus (Father of Taxonomy).
- Five Kingdom System (Whittaker & Margulis):
  1. Monera: Unicellular, prokaryotic, no nuclear membrane or nucleolus, peptidoglycan cell wall, binary fission (Bacteria, Cyanobacteria/Nostoc).
  2. Protista: Unicellular/colonial, eukaryotic, well-formed nucleus, mitosis/conjugation (Amoeba, Euglena, Paramecium, Diatoms).
  3. Fungi: Eukaryotic, mostly multicellular, heterotrophic/saprophytic, chitin cell wall, reproduce by spores (Yeast, Penicillium, Agaricus/Mushroom).
  4. Plantae: Eukaryotic, multicellular, autotrophic with chlorophyll, cellulose cell wall, advanced tissue systems (Bryophytes, Pteridophytes, Gymnosperms, Angiosperms).
  5. Animalia: Eukaryotic, multicellular, heterotrophic, no cell wall, plastid or large central vacuole, ingestive nutrition (Invertebrates, Chordates).
- Binomial Nomenclature: Two-part Latinized scientific names (Genus capitalized + specific epithet lowercase, italicized or underlined separately): Human (Homo sapiens), Tiger (Panthera tigris), Water lily (Nymphaea nouchali), Mango (Mangifera indica), Oriental Magpie-Robin (Copsychus saularis), Cholera bacterium (Vibrio cholerae).

## Chapter 2: Cells and Tissues of Organisms (জীবকোষ ও টিস্যু)
- Cell Structure & Organelles:
  - Cell Wall: Cellulose, hemicellulose, lignin; provides rigidity and shape (absent in animals).
  - Plasma Membrane: Fluid Mosaic Model (Singer & Nicolson) — phospholipid bilayer, intrinsic/extrinsic proteins, cholesterol, glycocalyx.
  - Protoplasm & Cytoplasm: Cytoplasmic organelles: Mitochondria (Powerhouse of the cell, double-membrane, cristae, matrix, produces ATP), Plastids (Chloroplasts for photosynthesis with grana/stroma, Chromoplasts for color, Leucoplasts for storage), Endoplasmic Reticulum (RER with ribosomes for protein synthesis, SER for lipid synthesis), Ribosomes (70S in prokaryotes, 80S in eukaryotes), Golgi apparatus (packaging & secretion), Lysosomes (suicide bags, phagocytosis), Centrosome/Centrioles (spindle fiber formation in animal cells), Vacuoles.
  - Nucleus: Nuclear membrane, nuclear pore, nucleoplasm, chromatin reticulum (DNA + histones), nucleolus.
- Plant Tissues: Meristematic tissue (apical, intercalary, lateral) vs Permanent tissue (Simple: Parenchyma, Collenchyma, Sclerenchyma; Complex: Xylem [tracheids, vessels, xylem parenchyma, xylem fibers] for water transport, Phloem [sieve tubes, companion cells, phloem parenchyma, phloem fibers] for food translocation).
- Animal Tissues:
  1. Epithelial Tissue: Squamous, cuboidal, columnar, ciliated, glandular (protection, absorption, secretion).
  2. Connective Tissue: Loose (areolar, adipose), dense (tendon, ligament), specialized/skeletal (cartilage, bone with osteocytes and Haversian system), fluid connective tissue (blood & lymph).
  3. Muscular Tissue: Striated/skeletal (voluntary), smooth/unstriated (involuntary), cardiac muscle (intercalated discs, involuntary rhythmic contractions).
  4. Nervous Tissue: Neurons (cell body, dendrites, axon with myelin sheath, synapse) and Neuroglia.

## Chapter 3: Cell Division (কোষ বিভাজন)
- Types of Cell Division:
  1. Amitosis (Direct division): In prokaryotes, bacteria, yeast, amoeba; nucleus constricts directly into two without spindle formation.
  2. Mitosis (Equational division): In somatic cells of body; produces 2 identical daughter cells with same chromosome number (2n -> 2n).
     - Stages:
       - Prophase: Chromosomes condense, dehydrate, thicken; nucleolus & nuclear membrane begin disintegrating.
       - Prometaphase: Spindle apparatus forms from poles with traction fibers; aster rays develop.
       - Metaphase: Chromosomes align at equatorial/metaphasic plate; centromeres attach to spindle fibers; maximum condensation.
       - Anaphase: Centromere divides; sister chromatids separate into daughter chromosomes and move towards opposite poles (V, L, J, I shapes: metacentric, submetacentric, acrocentric, telocentric).
       - Telophase: Chromosomes reach poles, uncoil into chromatin network, nuclear membrane and nucleolus re-form; cytokinesis occurs (cell plate in plants, cleavage furrow in animals).
  3. Meiosis (Reductional division): In germ mother cells / reproductive cells (2n -> n); reduces chromosome number by half; produces 4 haploid gametes/spores.
     - Significance: Crossing over during Pachytene of Prophase I creates genetic variation; maintains constant chromosome number generation after generation.

## Chapter 4: Bio-energetics (জীবনীশক্তি)
- ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate): The "Biological Energy Currency" / energy coin of the cell (ATP <=> ADP + Pi + 7.3 kcal/mol or 30.5 kJ/mol). Photophosphorylation and oxidative phosphorylation.
- Photosynthesis (6CO2 + 12H2O -> C6H12O6 + 6H2O + 6O2 in presence of Chlorophyll & Sunlight):
  - Light Dependent Phase: In thylakoid membranes; photolysis of water (2H2O -> 4H+ + 4e- + O2); reduction of NADP+ to NADPH + H+; ATP synthesis.
  - Light Independent Phase (Carbon Fixation): In stroma:
    1. C3 Pathway (Calvin-Benson Cycle): First stable product is 3-PGA (3-Phosphoglyceric acid). Catalyzed by RuBisCO. Found in rice, wheat, jute.
    2. C4 Pathway (Hatch-Slack Cycle): First stable product is 4-carbon Oxaloacetic acid (OAA). Found in maize, sugarcane, sorghum; higher photosynthetic efficiency.
  - Factors affecting photosynthesis: Light, CO2 (0.04% in air; optimum 1.0%), temperature (optimum 22 deg C to 35 deg C), water, minerals (Mg, Fe).
- Cellular Respiration (C6H12O6 + 6O2 -> 6CO2 + 6H2O + 38 ATP):
  - Aerobic Respiration Stages:
    1. Glycolysis: In cytoplasm; 1 Glucose -> 2 Pyruvic acid + 2 ATP + 2 NADH + H+ (no O2 required).
    2. Acetyl-CoA Formation: In mitochondrial matrix; 2 Pyruvic acid -> 2 Acetyl-CoA + 2 CO2 + 2 NADH + H+.
    3. Krebs Cycle (Citric Acid Cycle): In mitochondrial matrix; completes oxidation; yields CO2, ATP/GTP, NADH, FADH2.
    4. Electron Transport System (ETS): In inner mitochondrial membrane (cristae); oxidative phosphorylation yielding ATP and H2O.
  - Anaerobic Respiration: Alcoholic fermentation (C6H12O6 -> 2 C2H5OH + 2 CO2 + 2 ATP) in yeast vs Lactic acid fermentation (C6H12O6 -> 2 Lactic Acid + 2 ATP) in bacteria and fatigued muscle cells.

## Chapter 5: Food, Nutrition and Digestion (খাদ্য, পুষ্টি এবং পরিপাক)
- Six Food Constituents: Carbohydrates (energy, 4 kcal/g), Proteins (growth & repair, amino acids, 4 kcal/g), Lipids/Fats (stored energy, fatty acids + glycerol, 9 kcal/g), Vitamins (A, D, E, K fat-soluble; B-complex, C water-soluble), Minerals (Ca, P, Fe, I), Water.
- Body Mass Index (BMI): BMI = (Weight in kg) / (Height in meters)^2. Standard ranges: Underweight (<18.5), Normal (18.5-24.9), Overweight (25-29.9), Obese (>= 30).
- Human Digestive System & Digestion:
  - Alimentary canal: Mouth -> Buccal cavity -> Pharynx -> Esophagus -> Stomach -> Small intestine (Duodenum, Jejunum, Ileum) -> Large intestine (Caecum, Colon, Rectum) -> Anus.
  - Digestive enzymes:
    - Saliva: Salivary amylase (Ptyalin) converts starch to maltose.
    - Gastric juice (HCl + pepsinogen): HCl activates pepsin; Pepsin breaks proteins into peptones and proteoses.
    - Pancreatic juice: Trypsin/chymotrypsin (proteins -> peptides/amino acids), Pancreatic amylase (starch -> maltose), Pancreatic lipase (fats -> fatty acids + glycerol).
    - Liver & Bile: Bile emulsifies fats (mechanical breakdown without enzymes).
    - Intestinal juice: Erepsin, maltase, lactase, sucrase.
  - Absorption: Intestinal villi and microvilli; blood capillaries absorb glucose & amino acids; lacteals absorb fatty acids & glycerol.

## Chapter 6: Transport in Organisms (জীবে পরিবহন)
- Plant Water & Mineral Absorption: Imbibition (colloids), Osmosis (endosmosis/exosmosis across semipermeable membrane), Diffusion. Root hair absorption.
- Transpiration: Loss of water vapor from aerial plant parts. Types: Stomatal (90-95%), Cuticular (3-5%), Lenticular (1%). Stomatal mechanism governed by guard cells. Curtis called transpiration a "Necessary Evil". Ascent of sap via Xylem driven by transpiration pull, root pressure, cohesion-tension theory.
- Human Circulatory System:
  - Blood Components: Plasma (55% - water, albumin, globulin, fibrinogen) and Blood Corpuscles (45%):
    1. RBC (Erythrocytes): Biconcave, enucleated, hemoglobin, lifespan 120 days, transport O2 as oxyhemoglobin.
    2. WBC (Leukocytes): Nucleated, defensive soldiers; Granulocytes (Neutrophil, Eosinophil, Basophil), Agranulocytes (Lymphocyte produces antibodies, Monocyte phagocytosis).
    3. Platelets (Thrombocytes): Lifespan 5-10 days, initiate blood clotting via thromboplastin -> thrombin -> fibrin clot.
  - Blood Groups (ABO & Rh factor): A, B, AB (Universal recipient), O (Universal donor). Rh+ and Rh- incompatibility in pregnancy (Erythroblastosis fetalis).
  - Human Heart: 4 chambers (Right & Left Atria, Right & Left Ventricles). Tricuspid valve (right), Bicuspid/Mitral valve (left), Semilunar valves (pulmonary & aortic). Double circulation: Systemic & Pulmonary circulation. Blood pressure (Normal 120/80 mmHg; systolic/diastolic). Hypertension, heart attack, angiography, coronary bypass, pacemaker.

## Chapter 7: Gaseous Exchange (গ্যাসীয় বিনিময়)
- Gaseous Exchange in Plants: Stomata and lenticels exchange O2 and CO2 by simple diffusion based on partial pressure gradients.
- Human Respiratory System:
  - Pathway: External nostrils -> Nasal cavity -> Pharynx -> Larynx (Vocal cords) -> Trachea -> Bronchi -> Bronchioles -> Alveoli (air sacs surrounded by capillaries).
  - Mechanism of Breathing: Inspiration (Diaphragm flattens downward, intercostal muscles contract, ribs move upward and outward, thoracic volume increases, lung pressure drops, air rushes in) vs Expiration (muscles relax, thoracic volume decreases, air expelled).
  - Gas transport: Oxygen transported mainly as Oxyhemoglobin and dissolved in plasma. Carbon dioxide transported as bicarbonate ions (HCO3-), carbamino compounds, and dissolved carbonic acid.
  - Respiratory diseases: Asthma, Bronchitis, Emphysema, Pneumonia, Tuberculosis, Lung Cancer.

## Chapter 8: Excretion in Organisms (রেচন প্রক্রিয়া)
- Concept of Excretion: Elimination of nitrogenous metabolic wastes produced during cellular metabolism.
- Human Excretory System: Kidneys, Ureters, Urinary bladder, Urethra.
  - Structure of Kidney: Renal capsule, Cortex, Medulla, Renal pyramids, Renal pelvis.
  - Nephron (Structural & Functional Unit): ~1-1.2 million nephrons per kidney:
    1. Malpighian Body (Renal corpuscle): Glomerulus (tuft of capillaries from afferent arteriole) and Bowman's capsule (ultrafiltration).
    2. Renal Tubule: Proximal Convoluted Tubule (PCT - major reabsorption of water, glucose, Na+), Loop of Henle (descending and ascending limbs - countercurrent multiplier), Distal Convoluted Tubule (DCT), Collecting Duct.
  - Urine Formation: Glomerular ultrafiltration, tubular selective reabsorption, tubular secretion.
- Kidney Diseases & Remedies: Nephritis, kidney stones, renal failure. Dialysis (Hemodialysis using dialyzer with semipermeable cellophane tubing) and Kidney Transplantation.

## Chapter 9: Movement and Locomotion (দৃঢ়তা প্রদান ও চলন)
- Human Skeletal System: Total 206 bones in adult:
  - Axial Skeleton (80 bones): Skull (cranium 8, facial 14), Vertebral column (33 vertebrae: 7 cervical, 12 thoracic, 5 lumbar, 5 sacral, 4 coccygeal), Ribs (12 pairs) & Sternum.
  - Appendicular Skeleton (126 bones): Pectoral girdle & Upper limbs (arms), Pelvic girdle & Lower limbs (legs).
- Bone and Cartilage:
  - Bone: Rigid connective tissue with ossein matrix, calcium phosphate, osteocytes residing in lacunae connected by canaliculi around Haversian canals.
  - Cartilage: Flexible connective tissue with chondrin matrix, chondrocytes in lacunae (ear pinna, epiglottis, intervertebral discs, articular joints).
- Joints & Muscles: Fixed joints (sutures of skull), Slightly movable joints (vertebrae), Freely movable synovial joints (Ball & Socket joint of shoulder/hip, Hinge joint of elbow/knee). Tendons (bone-to-muscle dense fibrous tissue) vs Ligaments (bone-to-bone elastic tissue). Antagonistic muscle action (e.g. Biceps flexes arm, Triceps extends arm).

## Chapter 10: Coordination (সমন্বয়)
- Phytohormones (Plant Hormones): Auxin (apical dominance, phototropism), Gibberellin (internodal elongation, seed germination), Cytokinin (cell division), Abscisic acid & Ethylene (fruit ripening, leaf abscission).
- Human Nervous System:
  - Central Nervous System (CNS): Brain (Cerebrum for intelligence & voluntary actions, Cerebellum for muscular coordination & balance, Medulla oblongata for involuntary cardiac & respiratory control) and Spinal cord.
  - Peripheral Nervous System (PNS): Cranial nerves (12 pairs) and Spinal nerves (31 pairs).
  - Reflex Arc: Receptor -> Sensory neuron -> Interneuron in spinal cord -> Motor neuron -> Effector muscle.
- Endocrine System (Hormones):
  - Pituitary Gland (Master gland): Growth hormone, TSH, ACTH, Oxytocin, Vasopressin (ADH).
  - Thyroid Gland: Thyroxine (T4) and Triiodothyronine (T3) regulating BMR (Goitre caused by iodine deficiency).
  - Pancreas (Islets of Langerhans): Insulin (lowers blood glucose) and Glucagon (raises blood glucose). Diabetes Mellitus.
  - Adrenal Glands: Adrenaline (emergency fight-or-flight hormone).
  - Gonads: Testes (Testosterone) and Ovaries (Estrogen and Progesterone).

## Chapter 11: Reproduction in Organisms (জীবের প্রজনন)
- Plant Reproduction:
  - Structure of a complete flower: Calyx (sepals), Corolla (petals), Androecium (stamens: filament + anther with pollen grains), Gynoecium/Pistil (carpel: stigma, style, ovary with ovules).
  - Pollination: Self-pollination vs Cross-pollination (agents: wind, insects, birds, water).
  - Fertilization in Angiosperms: Double fertilization (1 sperm + egg cell -> 2n Zygote/Embryo; 1 sperm + secondary nucleus -> 3n Triploid Endosperm).
- Human Reproductive System:
  - Male: Testes (seminiferous tubules produce sperm, Leydig cells produce testosterone), Vas deferens, Prostate gland, Urethra.
  - Female: Ovaries (produce ova), Fallopian tubes (oviducts - site of fertilization), Uterus (endometrium - site of embryo implantation), Vagina. Menstrual cycle.
  - Development of Embryo: Zygote -> Cleavage -> Morula -> Blastocyst -> Implantation -> Placenta formation -> Gestation (~280 days / 40 weeks) -> Parturition (Birth).

## Chapter 12: Heredity in Organisms and Evolution (জীবের বংশগতি ও বিবর্তন)
- Hereditary Material: Chromosomes (Autosomes 22 pairs + Sex chromosomes 1 pair: XX in female, XY in male).
- DNA & RNA:
  - DNA (Deoxyribonucleic Acid): Double helix model by Watson & Crick (1953). Nucleotides composed of Deoxyribose sugar + Phosphate group + Nitrogenous bases: Purines (Adenine A, Guanine G), Pyrimidines (Thymine T, Cytosine C). Base pairing: A = T (2 hydrogen bonds), G = C (3 hydrogen bonds).
  - DNA Replication: Semiconservative replication (Meselson & Stahl).
  - RNA: Single-stranded; Uracil (U) replaces Thymine; Ribose sugar. Types: mRNA, tRNA, rRNA.
- Mendel's Laws of Inheritance (Gregor Johann Mendel):
  - First Law (Law of Segregation): Alleles separate during gamete formation; monohybrid phenotypic ratio 3:1, genotypic ratio 1:2:1.
  - Second Law (Law of Independent Assortment): Dihybrid phenotypic ratio 9:3:3:1.
- Evolution: Lamarckism (Use and disuse, inheritance of acquired traits) vs Darwinism (Charles Darwin, Origin of Species by Natural Selection: overproduction, struggle for existence, variations, survival of the fittest, speciation).

## Chapter 13: Environment of Life (জীবের পরিবেশ)
- Ecosystem: Structural and functional unit of ecology. Components:
  - Abiotic: Inorganic substances (C, N, H2O, O2), organic compounds (humus), climatic factors (temperature, light, wind, rainfall).
  - Biotic: Producers (autotrophic green plants), Consumers (Primary herbivores, Secondary carnivores, Tertiary top carnivores), Decomposers/Reducers (bacteria, fungi).
- Energy Flow in Ecosystem: Unidirectional flow following Laws of Thermodynamics; 10% law of energy transfer (Lindeman).
- Food Chains & Food Webs: Grazing food chain vs Detritus food chain. Trophic levels (T1 -> T2 -> T3 -> T4).
- Ecological Pyramids: Pyramid of Numbers, Pyramid of Biomass, Pyramid of Energy (always upright).
- Symbiotic Relationships: Mutualism (+/+, e.g. Lichen, Rhizobium in legume roots), Commensalism (+/0, e.g. Epiphytic orchids), Parasitism (+/-, e.g. Cuscuta, tapeworm).

## Chapter 14: Biotechnology (জীবপ্রযুক্তি)
- Definition & Applications: Application of biological organisms, systems, or processes to manufacturing and service industries.
- Plant Tissue Culture (Totipotency):
  - Concept: Ability of a plant cell to develop into a full plant.
  - Steps: Explant selection -> Culture medium preparation (nutrients, agar, phytohormones) -> Sterilization -> Inoculation -> Callus formation -> Shoot & root regeneration -> Acclimatization in soil.
- Recombinant DNA Technology (Genetic Engineering):
  - Steps:
    1. Isolation of target desired gene from donor organism.
    2. Selection and isolation of bacterial plasmid vector (e.g. E. coli plasmid).
    3. Cleavage of DNA and plasmid at specific palindrome sites using Restriction Endonucleases ("Molecular Scissors").
    4. Splicing/joining target gene into plasmid using DNA Ligase enzyme ("Molecular Glue").
    5. Transformation: Introduction of recombinant plasmid into host bacteria.
    6. Clonal proliferation and expression of the transformed recombinant bacteria.
  - Applications: Production of human insulin, vaccines, disease-resistant and drought-resistant transgenic crops (Bt-cotton, Golden Rice rich in Vitamin A, insect-resistant crops).
